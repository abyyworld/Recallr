All skills and where you are

A new skill starts with a 10-second lesson, then Predict, Fill the gaps and Write it. Not right? Try again as often as you like; the answer only appears when you give up, and the skill steps down a level. Right first time and it climbs up. After 3 goes in a day it waits until tomorrow. Essentials and All course questions share progress; Variations keep their own. A skill you can write comes back after 10 minutes, then 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days. "Mastered" means you wrote it correctly three times in a row, spread over days. A day counts toward your streak with at least 3 cards. Every 7 days earns a rest day (up to 2).

Sync between your phone and laptop

Off. Progress stays in this browser only.

Recallr has no server, so it syncs through your own GitHub account: your progress is kept in a private gist (a hidden file only you can see), and every device you connect reads and writes it.

  1. Create a GitHub token with only the gist box ticked, set "Expiration" to what you like, and copy it.
  2. Paste it here and press Connect. Do the same on your other device with the same token.
